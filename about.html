About ShareSchooling

Most homeschooling families no longer go it alone. They “shareschool”: teaming up with co-ops, tutors, churches and online options so no one has to carry everything by themselves.

If you've chosen to homeschool, you made the right decision. You don't have to know everything or teach every subject. Help is available, and you can do this.

What this site does

ShareSchooling is building a guide to homeschool resources, organized by state, county and city, so you can find the people and programs that can help your family. We're starting with a pilot in West Tennessee and DeSoto County, Mississippi.

It isn't a planner or record-keeping tool. It's here to connect you with help.

How listings work

Listings are gathered from public pages, and each one shows where the information came from and when we last checked it. Organizations can claim their listing to keep it up to date. Basic listings are free, and featured listings are always clearly labeled.
