    HomeLife Academy

    Umbrella schools · Jackson

    A Christian, Category IV church-related umbrella school with administrative offices in Jackson. It keeps records, issues transcripts and diplomas, and offers counseling. It reports enrollment to each county and does not require testing.

    Tennessee Home Education Association (THEA)

    State associations and legal help

    A statewide, Christian-led homeschool association since 1984 that monitors legislation, hosts Rally Day at the Capitol, and coordinates regional chapters (MHEA and WTHEA in West TN).

    • Statewide
    • Online
    • Faith-based

    Last verified: Oct 27, 2026

    • Draft

    Tennessee Individualized Education Account (IEA) Program

    Special needs and learning-difference support

    A state program that can fund approved curriculum, tutoring, therapies, and technology for eligible K-12 students with qualifying disabilities who leave public school, including homeschoolers.

    • Statewide
    • Online
    • Secular

    Last verified: Oct 27, 2026

    Titus 2 Academy

    Umbrella schools

    A Tennessee Category IV church-related umbrella school that provides legal covering, attendance reporting to the board of education, online record keeping, and academic counseling for homeschool families statewide.
