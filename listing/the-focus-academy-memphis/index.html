The FOCUS Academy

Microschools

A microschool for gifted and neurodivergent (twice-exceptional) middle school boys, offering 2-day or 4-day options that blend self-paced academics with hands-on STEM mini-studies. Students are registered as independent homeschoolers or under an umbrella school.
