First Regional Library: DeSoto County branches

Curriculum and online programs

The regional public library system, with branches in Hernando, Horn Lake, Olive Branch, Southaven, and Walls. It runs free children's and teen programs (storytimes, LEGO club, art, live animals) and a summer reading program.
