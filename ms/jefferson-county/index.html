    Mississippi Home Educators Association (MHEA)

    State associations and legal help

    A statewide Christian homeschool association that monitors legislation, hosts Capitol Day and Camp Dixon, and keeps a county-by-county list of affiliated support groups. Not the same group as the Memphis Home Education Association.
